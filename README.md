# Project title
- ESP32 Expansion Board
---
## Description
 The ESP32 Expansion Board is designed around the ESP32-DEVKIT-V1 module to provide easy access to the microcontroller's GPIO pins and simplify interfacing with external devices. The board includes a regulated power supply section, reset circuitry, and multiple expansion headers for connecting sensors, actuators, and communication modules.
---

## Features
- ESP32 DEVKIT V1
- Supply Voltage: Regulated +5 V
- Wireless Communication: Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n) and Bluetooth/BLE
- GPIO Expansion: Multiple 15-pin headers
-  Reset Function: Push-button switch
-  Communication Interfaces: UART, SPI, I²C
-  Analog Inputs: ESP32 ADC channels
-  PWM Outputs: Supported through ESP32 GPIO pins
---

## Components
- 1 × ESP32-DEVKIT-V1 Module
- 1 × AMS1117-5.0 Voltage Regulator
- 1 × Push Button Switch
- 1 × 1 kΩ Resistor
- 2 × 100 nF Ceramic Capacitors
- 2 × 1 µF Capacitors
- 6 × 15-Pin Female Headers
- PCB and power connectors
